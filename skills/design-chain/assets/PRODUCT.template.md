## Platform

web | ios | android | adaptive

## Users

Who uses it, in what situation, doing which job. List secondary audiences only if confirmed.

## Product purpose

What it makes possible, why it exists, what success looks like.

## Positioning

What this product does, or can honestly claim, that a close competitor could not.

## Operating context

Where and how people actually use or evaluate it: workflows, places, devices, documents, routines.

## Capabilities and constraints

What it does today, technical limits, the exact terms to use, and product decisions still open.

## Brand commitments

Name, voice, assets and identity constraints the owner has made binding.

## Evidence on hand

Real content, data, screenshots, testimonials, case studies, photography, with
paths. List what does not exist yet, so no session invents it.

## Product principles

Three to five durable strategic principles. No visual recipes.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Known user needs or the required standard.

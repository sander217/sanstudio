## Platform

web | ios | android | adaptive

## Users

Who uses it, in what situation, doing which job. Add other audiences only when
confirmed.

## Product purpose

What it makes possible, why it exists, what success looks like.

## Positioning

The mechanism or claim a neighboring product could not truthfully copy.

## Operating context

Workflows, environments, devices, documents and rituals that are factual parts
of using or evaluating the product.

## Capabilities and constraints

Confirmed functionality, technical limits, terminology to use exactly, and
open product decisions.

## Brand commitments

Name, voice, assets and identity constraints the owner has made binding.

## Evidence on hand

Real content, data, screenshots, testimonials, case studies, photography, with
paths. List what does not exist yet, so no session invents it.

## Product principles

Three to five durable strategic principles. No visual recipes.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Known user needs or the required standard.
